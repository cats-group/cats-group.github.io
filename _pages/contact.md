For general inquiries about CATS research or papers, please email [Julia Mendelsohn](mailto:juliame@umd.edu). If you do not hear back within a week, please feel free to send a follow-up email.


## Prospective students

Thank you for being interested in working with us! 

The lab is currently full, so we're unfortunately not accepting new Bachelors students, Masters students, or external visitors at this time. 

Prospective PhD students should apply to the [PhD in Information Studies program at UMD](https://info.umd.edu/academics/doctoral-programs/phd-information-studies/). If you are a current UMD PhD student interested in working with us, please email Julia to set up a meeting.
